## W24 - Winter 2024

### Overview
- **Total Companies**: 260 companies
- **Applications**: Over 27,000
- **Acceptance Rate**: Under 1% (one of the most selective in YC history)
- **Demo Day**: April 3-4, 2024
- **AI Concentration**: 50-70% building around AI

### Key Statistics
- 59% applied more than once
- 52% accepted with only an idea
- 21% have a woman founder
- 11% of founders are women

### Focus Areas
- B2B/Enterprise SaaS: 54%
- DevTools: 17%
- Fintech: 12%

### Common Themes
- Open source
- Dev tools
- AI

### Notable Companies

#### AI & Automation
- Magic Hour (content generation, editing & production)
- Artisan AI (building digital workers called Artisans)
- Maihem (AI agents for evaluating AI products)
- Basepilot (AI coworkers that learn from demonstrations)

- Taiki (API for financial forms and data extraction)

## S23 - Summer 2023

### Overview
- **Total Companies**: 229 startups (some sources cite ~220)
- **Applications**: Over 24,000
- **Acceptance Rate**: Less than 1% (lowest in YC history at the time)
- **AI Concentration**: Over 60% classified as AI startups

### Key Statistics
- 75% began with no revenue
- 81% had not previously raised any funding

### Focus Areas
- B2B SaaS/Enterprise: 70%
- Fintech: 10%
- Healthcare: 10%
- Consumer: 6%
- Proptech/Industrials: 4%

### Notable Theme
AI had a significant presence in the S23 class, with YC noting that "recent developments in AI have unlocked an entire universe of possibilities, and there has never been a better time to start an AI company."

## Key Trends Across All 6 Batches

### AI Dominance
The concentration of AI companies has grown dramatically:
- W23: 40%
- S23: 60%+
- W24: 50-70%
- S24: 67-75%
- W25: 82%
- S25: 88%

### Geographic Concentration
- San Francisco continues to dominate, with concentration increasing over time
- S24 saw 67% in SF, up from 58% in W24

### Selectivity
- Acceptance rates have remained below 1% for most recent batches
- Application volumes: 20,000-27,000+ per batch

### Founder Characteristics
- Strong technical backgrounds (72% with CS background in S24)
- High percentage of advanced degrees (55% with Master's or Ph.D. in S24)
- Increasing diversity (21% woman founder representation in W24)

### Funding Status
- Many companies accepted pre-revenue (75% in S23)
- Most accepted without prior funding (81% in S23)
- 52% accepted with just an idea (W23, W24)
